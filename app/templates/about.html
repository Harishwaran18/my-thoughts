Welcome to my corner of the internet. This is where I write about the things I'm thinking about — technology, learning, books, and the slow craft of building things that last.

Why I write

Writing is how I think. When I try to explain an idea in words, I find the gaps in my understanding. This blog is a public notebook: a place to work out ideas, share what I learn, and occasionally say something worth your time.

The best way to learn something deeply is to teach it — or better, to write about it where someone might read it.

What you'll find here

  • Essays — longer reflections on topics I keep returning to.
  • Notes — short observations, links, and half-formed ideas.
  • Build logs — things I'm making and what I learn along the way.

Stay in touch

If anything here resonates, I'd love to hear from you. Reach out, share a post, or just say hello. You can use the share buttons at the bottom of each article, or start a conversation wherever you find me online.

This blog is built with Flask, Markdown, and SQLite — simple tools, kept intentionally small.
